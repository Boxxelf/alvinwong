About

Alvin Wong is a high school senior at Eleanor Roosevelt High School in Eastvale, California, working in UX design — Figma, wireframing, prototyping, and building user-friendly digital products.

He designs end to end: research first, then FigJam wireframes, then prototypes people can actually click through. Lately that means Catalyst, a career navigation platform, and Card Binder, an iOS app for trading card collectors.

Off screen he is a magician and Magic Club officer. Performing taught him the thing design keeps reminding him of — the audience decides whether it worked. He is looking for internship, volunteer, and part-time opportunities.
